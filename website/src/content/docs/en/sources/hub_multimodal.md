# Document Hub and Multimodal Formats

The **Document Hub** centralizes all your study materials in one place and preserves a direct link between every flashcard and the exact paragraph it came from.

## 1. Supported Study Sources

| Source Type | Formats | What AnkiDraft Extracts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PDF Textbooks & Handouts** | `.pdf` | Chapter outline, text, tables, figures, and LaTeX math |
| **Online Videos** | YouTube URLs | Timestamped subtitles or local speech-to-text transcription |
| **Audio Recordings** | `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a` | Paragraph-segmented transcript |
| **Web Articles** | `https://` URLs | Clean article body without ads or navigation clutter |
| **Office & Ebooks** | `.docx`, `.pptx`, `.epub`, `.md` | Heading hierarchy, lists, and slide notes |

## 2. Jump Back to the Source in One Click

While reviewing or editing any flashcard in the editor, click **View Source** to jump straight back to the corresponding chapter and paragraph in your original document.
