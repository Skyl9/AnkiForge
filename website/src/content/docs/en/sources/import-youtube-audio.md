# Importing YouTube Videos and Audio Lectures

AnkiDraft lets you build flashcards directly from online video lectures or recorded classroom sessions.

## 1. Importing a YouTube Video

1. Open the **Documents** view and click **Import from URL / YouTube**.
2. Paste the YouTube video link.
3. AnkiDraft immediately retrieves official or auto-generated subtitles and reconstructs a clean, timestamped transcript organized by topic.
4. If the video has no subtitles available, AnkiDraft can transcribe the audio stream locally.

## 2. Transcribing Audio Files (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.m4a`)

Recorded a lecture or voice memo?
1. Drop your audio file into the **Documents** view.
2. Automatic transcription splits the recording into timestamped paragraphs.
3. Generate flashcards from the entire recording or pick only the key segments.
