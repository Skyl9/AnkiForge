# Importing PDFs, Slides, and Ebooks

The AnkiDraft **Document Hub** turns static study files into structured chapters ready for flashcard generation.

## 1. Supported File Formats

Drag and drop directly into the **Documents** view:
- **PDF Documents (`.pdf`)**: lecture handouts, academic papers, and textbooks, preserving tables, figures, and LaTeX math equations.
- **Word Documents (`.docx`) and Slides (`.pptx`)**: extracts headings, bullet points, and speaker notes.
- **Ebooks (`.epub`) and Markdown / Plain Text (`.md`, `.txt`)**.

## 2. Choosing the PDF Extraction Mode

When importing a PDF, two extraction modes are available depending on your file:
- **Fast Standard Extraction**: best for clean digital PDFs where text is already selectable.
- **Advanced OCR & Math Recognition**: recommended for scanned handouts, multi-column layouts, or math-heavy courses.

## 3. Keeping Diagrams and Figures

Figures and diagrams extracted from your documents are automatically saved to your local media library so you can attach them to the front or back of any flashcard in one click.
