# Importing a Deck (.apkg) or Collection (.colpkg)

Already using Anki for months or years? You can bring your existing flashcards into AnkiDraft anytime.

## 1. Opening the Import Wizard (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+I</kbd>)

Press <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+I</kbd> and drop your file: AnkiDraft automatically detects whether it is an `.apkg` deck package or a full `.colpkg` collection archive.

## 2. Difference Between `.apkg` and `.colpkg`

- **`.apkg` Deck Package (Incremental Merge)**: adds flashcards shared by a classmate or updates a specific deck without touching the rest of your collection.
- **`.colpkg` Collection Archive (Full Sync from Anki)**: aligns all your decks, note types, and review counters with your current Anki collection while preserving your PDFs, settings, and AI assistants inside AnkiDraft.

For a visual walkthrough of how AnkiDraft and Anki work together, read the [Visual Guide to the Distributed Lifecycle](/en/guides/cycle_de_vie_distribue/).
