# Quickstart Guide

This step-by-step guide walks you through creating, reviewing, and exporting your very first Anki flashcard deck in under 5 minutes.

## The 5-Step Workflow

1. **Import study material**: drop a PDF textbook, YouTube lecture, or web article into the **Documents** view.
2. **Select the sections to study**: check the specific chapters or pages you want to cover.
3. **Generate flashcard drafts**: let AI extract atomic question/answer cards or write your own.
4. **Review and check quality**: refine wording, add math formulas or audio, and run the Wozniak quality linter.
5. **Export to Anki**: generate an `.apkg` package ready to open in Anki Desktop or AnkiMobile.

## Step 1: Select or Create Your Profile

On first launch, AnkiDraft opens the `default` profile. If you want to keep subjects completely separate (e.g., *Medicine*, *Languages*), click the profile selector to create an isolated workspace.

## Step 2: Configure an AI Provider (Optional)

Open **Settings** (<kbd>Ctrl+,</kbd>) and select the **AI Providers** tab:
- **100% Free & Local**: install [Ollama](https://ollama.com) on your computer and pick a local model.
- **Cloud API Key**: enter your OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude key. Your key is stored encrypted inside your operating system keychain.

## Step 3: Import Your First Document

1. Open the **Documents** view from the sidebar.
2. Drag and drop a lecture PDF or paste a YouTube video URL.
3. Once the document is split into chapters, click **Generate Cards** to start your first extraction.

## Step 4: Validate and Export to Anki

Review the proposed flashcards, adjust them if needed, and press <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+E</kbd> (**Export Anki Cards (.apkg)**). Double-click the exported `.apkg` file to load your new cards directly into Anki!
