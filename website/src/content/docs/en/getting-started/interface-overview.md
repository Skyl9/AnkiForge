# Interface Overview

AnkiDraft is designed to stay clean and responsive whether you work on a laptop screen or an ultrawide monitor.

## 1. Main Workspaces

Switch between workspaces from the sidebar or with keyboard shortcuts <kbd>Ctrl+1</kbd> through <kbd>Ctrl+6</kbd>:

- **Dashboard (<kbd>Ctrl+1</kbd>)**: overview of your decks, document coverage progress, and recent activity.
- **Editor & Browser (<kbd>Ctrl+2</kbd>)**: manual creation, editing with live KaTeX math preview, cloze deletions, and card management.
- **Documents & Sources (<kbd>Ctrl+3</kbd>)**: library of your PDFs, YouTube videos, audio recordings, and web articles with per-chapter coverage bars.
- **Generation & Pipelines (<kbd>Ctrl+4</kbd>)**: batch flashcard creation across selected chapters.
- **Quality & Wozniak Audit (<kbd>Ctrl+5</kbd>)**: detection of overloaded, vague, or hard-to-memorize flashcards.
- **AI Consultant (<kbd>Ctrl+6</kbd>)**: conversational assistant that inspects your decks and prepares staged improvements for your approval.

## 2. Command Palette (<kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd>)

Press <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd> anytime to open the global command palette: type a few letters to jump to a view, trigger an `.apkg` export, open a user guide, or change a setting without leaving the keyboard.

## 3. Customizing Layouts and Themes

In **Settings** (<kbd>Ctrl+,</kbd>) > **Appearance**:
- Pick from **4 interchangeable layouts** (*IDE Studio*, *macOS Native*, *Dashboard*, *Glassmorphism*) using high-resolution preview cards.
- Choose from **12 color themes** (light and dark) crafted for long study sessions.
